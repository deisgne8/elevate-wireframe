Company introduction

A developer guided by how people live.

ELEVATE is a UAE-based real estate developer focused on thoughtfully designed, lifestyle-led residential environments. We look beyond individual buildings to consider how architecture, wellbeing, hospitality and community can support a fuller everyday life.

Backed by the capabilities and long-term perspective of One Group, ELEVATE brings together specialist partners around a clear residential vision.
