Privacy Policy

How privacy information will be organized for visitors and people who contact ELEVATE.

1. Who we are

The final policy will identify the legal entity operating this website and the contact details for privacy-related enquiries.

2. Information provided by visitors

We may collect the name, email address, phone number, enquiry preferences and messages provided through enquiry and newsletter forms.

3. How information is used

Explain how information supports responses to enquiries, appointment requests, subscribed updates and website improvement.

4. Service providers & lead management

Identify the approved CRM, hosting, analytics and communications providers. The sitemap mentions Zoho; the production integration and routing require confirmation.

5. Cookies & preferences

Essential and optional cookies, their purposes and available controls are described on the Cookie Preferences page.

6. Retention, security & choices

Set out approved retention periods, security practices and the available process for privacy requests, unsubscribing and withdrawing optional consent.

7. Contact & updates

Approved privacy contact details, policy effective date and update process will be supplied before publication.
