A closer look at island living.

The design and lifestyle ideas behind Mondrian Al Marjan Island Beach Residences.

08 October 2026 · Project updates

Project editorial hero / placeholder

A residential experience with a sense of place.

Set on Al Marjan Island in Ras Al Khaimah, ELEVATE’s flagship development connects its residential vision with the hospitality perspective of Ennismore and the design-led identity of Mondrian.

Our philosophy connects Health, Wellness, Tribe, Purpose and Flow with a considered approach to development. Each project offers an opportunity to express these ideas through architecture, shared spaces and everyday experience.
