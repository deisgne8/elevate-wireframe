Architecture & interiors

Landscape and living as one.

Inspired by smooth, sculpted pebbles shaped by time and tide, the architecture and interiors bring balance, tactile materiality and a close relationship with the natural setting.

Gensler leads the architecture, with interiors by Bergman Design House.

The location

An iconic beachfront setting.

Al Marjan Island combines pristine beaches and panoramic views with access to Ras Al Khaimah’s hospitality, leisure and cultural destinations.

  • Wynn Al Marjan Island — 7 minutes
  • Al Hamra Mall — 11 minutes
  • RAK Central — 12 minutes
  • Ras Al Khaimah International Airport — 35 minutes
  • Dubai International Airport — approximately 1 hour
